I am currently a member of the <a href='https://research.coe.drexel.edu/ece/imaple/'>iMaPLe Research Lab</a>, where I am pursuing a Ph.D. in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Drexel University under Dr. David Han’s supervision. Before joining iMaPLe, I received my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology in 2021, and my M.Sc. in Robotics from Worcester Polytechnic Institute in 2023. During my M.Sc. I also worked as a Software Engineer at Superlative Technologies Inc, a contractor for the U.S. Transportation Command. 

My research interests are in perception and robotics, especially how deep learning and computer vision can make robotic systems more capable in the real world. I have been fortunate enough to be able to explore a lot of research areas, and recently I have started to narrow my focus to work on methods for spatial understanding in robotics.

Apart from research, I have a wide variety of hobbies and interests. I like solving Rubik's cubes, and I average 9 seconds using the Roux method. I compete regularly in <a href='https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/persons/2016PETE11'>WCA competitions</a>, and I also built and maintain <a href='https://www.rouxcubing.com'>rouxcubing.com</a>, a site with resources for solving using the Roux method. Besides cubing, some of my other active hobbies include <a href='https://www.youtube.com/@Robotin30Hours'>30-hour robot builds</a>, photography (digital and film), printmaking, blacksmithing, reading, and ultimate frisbee.
